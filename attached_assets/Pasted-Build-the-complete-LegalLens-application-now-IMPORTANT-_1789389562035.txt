==================================================
PRODUCT
==================================================

Name:

LegalLens

Slogan:

"Eat smart. Pay fair. Know your food."

LegalLens is a mobile-first consumer web application that lets someone photograph a packaged food product and understand:

- what is written on the package
- whether important label information appears to be present
- the real unit price
- nutrition information
- sugar-related ingredients

The original project concept is based on automatically detecting, extracting and validating mandatory declarations on packaged products under Legal Metrology rules.

The prototype also adds consumer-friendly nutrition and sugar analysis.

The application should feel like a polished food-tech consumer product.

It must NOT look like:
- a government website
- an enterprise dashboard
- a developer tool
- a generic AI chatbot
- Iron Man/JARVIS

Use a warm, premium, food-oriented visual design.
